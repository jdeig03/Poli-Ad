Answers in the Candidate's Own Words.

Poli only uses what your candidate has actually said and published. No spin.

P
Maya's AI assistant SAMPLE
Did she really vote to cut the library budget?
In 2020 Maya voted to reduce the library system's capital budget by 12%, redirecting those funds to deferred maintenance. She has since said she'd restore capital funding if re-elected.
