# POLI — Instagram Reel/Story Ad Script & Visual Storyboard
**"See Through It"**
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 | **Length:** 21 seconds | **Platform:** Instagram Reels/Stories

---

## OVERVIEW

**Concept:** A visual interrogation. The first half is an accusation leveled at the entire political information ecosystem — fast, aggressive, no mercy. The second half hands the viewer a tool. The tone shift is a crack of light in a dark room. Not a fade. A switch.

**Visual Language:** Black or near-black backgrounds throughout. White type as the default. Red is used *only* for contradiction/failure states — it means something is wrong, exposed, caught. Poli's brand accent (electric teal `#00F5D4`) appears *only* in the second half — it is associated exclusively with clarity, evidence, truth. The viewer learns the color system without being told.

## 1. SHOT-BY-SHOT STORYBOARD

---

### SHOT 1 — `0:00–0:02`
**On-Screen Text:**
```
THEY SAID ONE THING.
```
*(Large, uppercase, white, condensed sans-serif — e.g. Bebas Neue or Barlow Condensed. The text slams in from the left on a single cut, no fade. Characters land hard like a punch.)*

**Visual Direction:**
Pure black background. Text occupies 70% of screen width, vertically centered. On entry: letters smash in left-to-right with a subtle horizontal motion blur, like they're being thrown. A single frame of digital noise/static flickers at the very end of the shot, just before cut.

**Voiceover:**
> *"They said one thing."*
[flat, slow, deliberate — like you already know what's coming]

**Sound/Music:**
Music cold start — a single deep, low sub-bass tone, almost felt more than heard. Sits under everything like pressure building.

---

### SHOT 2 — `0:02–0:04`
**On-Screen Text:**
```
THEN THEY DID
THE OPPOSITE.
```
*(Same typeface. "THE OPPOSITE." is 20% larger than the line above it. On the word "OPPOSITE" — a flash of RED bleeds into the letters before they snap back to white.)*

**Visual Direction:**
Background stays black. Text enters with a rapid vertical slam — drops from above like a guillotine. "THE OPPOSITE." lands with a micro screen-shake (2–3px, single frame). The red color bleed on "OPPOSITE" lasts exactly 4 frames — subliminal but registrable.

**Voiceover:**
> *"Then did the exact opposite."*
[slightly faster, the edge of contempt starting to show]

**Sound/Music:**
Sub-bass tightens. A very subtle high-frequency whine begins layering in underneath, almost imperceptible. This is the tension building.

---

### SHOT 3 — `0:04–0:06`
**On-Screen Text:**
```
AND NOBODY
CALLED IT OUT.
```
*(Text enters as a glitch — the letters appear but fragmented, displaced, then snap into correct position. Like corrupted data resolving. Feels like a system trying to correct itself and barely managing.)*

**Visual Direction:**
Background shifts from pure black to a very dark charcoal (#0D0D0D) — barely perceptible, but the world is getting slightly dirtier. Glitch animation: 3–4 rapid frames where letter positions are offset by 8–20px before snapping to grid. Small horizontal scan lines flicker across the screen once during this shot.

**Voiceover:**
> *"And nobody called it out."*
[flat again, but heavier — the contempt has settled into something colder]

**Sound/Music:**
A single short glitch/digital artifact sound effect coincides with the text glitch. Sharp, electronic, wrong-sounding. Then back to the low sub-bass.

---

### SHOT 4 — `0:06–0:08`
**On-Screen Text:**
```
NOT THE MEDIA.
NOT THE PARTY.
NOT ANYONE.
```
*(Three lines, each entering on a rapid stagger — 3 frames between each. White. Then, as the third line lands, all three lines flash bright RED simultaneously for 8 frames before cutting to black.)*

**Visual Direction:**
Background: pure black again. Text is stacked, centered, slightly tighter leading than normal — cramped and urgent. The three-line stagger should feel like three separate accusations landing in rapid succession. The simultaneous red flash at the end is the emotional peak of the first half — it should feel like an alarm going off.

**Voiceover:**
> *"Not the media. Not the party. Not anyone."*
[building energy — each word "Not" lands harder than the last. Fast. Angry. Not shouting — controlled anger.]

**Sound/Music:**
Each "Not" line coincides with a sharp low percussion hit — not a snare, something deeper, like a single hit on a floor tom or a 808 kick. Three hits. Then on the red flash: a sharp, discordant high-end sting — one note, no reverb, harsh.

---

### SHOT 5 — `0:08–0:10`
**On-Screen Text:**
```
YOU JUST HAD TO
FIGURE IT OUT
YOURSELF.
```
*(Text is slightly larger than all previous shots — maximum urgency. "YOURSELF." is isolated on its own line, the largest word on screen so far. It enters last, a half-beat after the others, and just... sits there.)*

**Visual Direction:**
Background: pure black. This is the moment the accusation turns toward the viewer. The text size escalation makes this feel like the volume is rising without the volume rising. "YOURSELF." enters with a rapid zoom-in that starts at 120% scale and snaps down to 100% in 3 frames — aggressive, in your face.

**Voiceover:**
> *"You just had to figure it out yourself."*
[drops slightly in volume — not softer, but more intimate. Almost like someone leaning forward and saying this directly to you. The word "yourself" is enunciated clearly.]

**Sound/Music:**
Music begins a rapid build — a tight, rising swell. Not melodic. Textural. The high-frequency whine from earlier comes fully into audible range now, almost uncomfortable.

---

### ⚡ THE BEAT — `0:10–0:11.5`

**On-Screen Text:**
*(Nothing. The screen goes completely black. One full second. No text. No animation.)*

**Visual Direction:**
Complete blackout. This is not a fade — it is a hard cut to pure black, then a hard cut out of it. No transition. The absence should feel intentional and slightly startling after the barrage. This is the visual equivalent of a pause before a sentence that changes everything.

**Voiceover:**
*(Silence. One beat. A breath.)*

**Sound/Music:**
ALL MUSIC DROPS. Complete silence. This silence lasts the full 1.5 seconds. It should feel like a room suddenly going quiet. The contrast with what just happened is everything.

---

### SHOT 6 — `0:11.5–0:14`
**On-Screen Text:**
```
POLI
```
*(The word "POLI" enters in the brand teal — `#00F5D4`. First appearance of color in the ad. It does not slam or glitch. It fades up over 12 frames — gentle, intentional, confident. Not aggressive. The first thing in this ad that isn't aggressive. That's the point.)*

**Visual Direction:**
Background lifts from pure black to a very deep dark navy/charcoal (#080C10) — not light, but a tiny amount of depth. The teal wordmark centers on screen. Underneath it, in small white text, a subtitle fades up 18 frames after "POLI":

```
      political truth. ranked. sourced.
```
*(Lowercase, lighter weight than the all-caps text used throughout. The case change matters — it's a different register. More precise. More measured.)*

The POLI wordmark should have a very subtle glow — teal light diffusing outward about 20px, as if it's emitting something. Not neon-sign loud. Just present.

**Voiceover:**
> *"Poli."*
[single word. quiet. let it land. a beat.] *"Political truth. Ranked. Sourced."*
[measured, deliberate — three short declarations, not a sales pitch]

**Sound/Music:**
New music enters — but it's different. Cleaner. A single sustained synth tone, teal-colored in feeling, that begins building underneath the voiceover. Lower in tempo than what came before. Purposeful.

---

### SHOT 7 — `0:14–0:17`
**On-Screen Text:**
```
SEE EXACTLY WHERE
THEY CONTRADICTED
THEMSELVES.
```
*(White text, same condensed typeface. But animation style has changed: text now builds left-to-right, letter by letter, fast — like it's being typed/revealed rather than thrown. It feels like information being surfaced. Like something being uncovered.)*

Below, in teal, slightly smaller:
```
Every contradiction.
Every source.
Every time.
```
*(These three lines appear one at a time, staggered, each preceded by a small teal checkmark icon — just a mark, no word "verified" anywhere. The check marks do the work.)*

**Visual Direction:**
Background stays dark. The transition from the POLI logo shot should be a clean, smooth cut — not a slam. As each teal line appears, a faint teal horizontal line sweeps across the background from left to right behind the text, like a scan — very subtle, like something being read.

**Voiceover:**
> *"See exactly where they contradicted themselves."*
[clear, controlled — not angry anymore. Empowered. Like explaining something you know is true.] *(half beat)* *"Every contradiction. Every source. Every time."*
[each phrase lands with finality — not rushed]

**Sound/Music:**
Synth tone from the previous shot continues building. The teal check lines coincide with soft, clean, high-pitched chime tones — three of them, one per line. Not cute or app-like. Precise. Clinical. Satisfying.

---

### SHOT 8 — `0:17–0:19`
**On-Screen Text:**
```
THE PROOF IS THERE.
YOU JUST NEEDED
A WAY TO SEE IT.
```
*(White text. The phrase "A WAY TO SEE IT." glows faintly teal at the end — not fully teal, but the white has a barely-perceptible teal tinge. As if the text itself has been clarified.)*

**Visual Direction:**
This is the slowest shot in the ad. Text enters at normal pace — no slamming, no glitch. The background pulses once — a single slow, wide ripple of deep teal light emanating from the center of the screen, expanding outward like a sonar ping, then fading. Happens once, timed to the word "see."

**Voiceover:**
> *"The proof has always been there. You just needed a way to see it."*
[low, warm — the anger is gone. What's here now is something like vindication. Like: we got you.]

**Sound/Music:**
Music swells to its fullest point — not loud, but full. The synth tone resolves into a minor-to-major shift: tension releasing. Not triumphant. Just... clear.

## 2. VOICEOVER SCRIPT

**Full script with delivery notes:**

---

*"They said one thing."*
[flat, slow, deliberate — like you already know what's coming]

*"Then did the exact opposite."*
[slightly faster — contempt entering the edges]

*"And nobody called it out."*
[flat, heavier — contempt settling into something colder]

*"Not the media. Not the party. Not anyone."*
[building — each "Not" harder than the last. Fast. Controlled anger, not shouting]

*"You just had to figure it out yourself."*
[drops to intimate — leaning forward, talking to one person. "Yourself" lands clearly]

*— [one beat of silence] —*

*"Poli."*
[single word. quiet. let it sit for a half-beat.]

*"Political truth. Ranked. Sourced."*
[measured, deliberate — three short declarations. Not a pitch. A statement.]

*"See exactly where they contradicted themselves."*
[clear, controlled — empowered, not angry]

*"Every contradiction. Every source. Every time."*
[each phrase has finality — not rushed]

*"The proof has always been there. You just needed a way to see it."*
[low, warm — vindication. Almost private.]

---

**Casting/Delivery Notes:**
- Voice should read as a real person, not a narrator. Slightly rough around the edges — not produced-smooth.
- Gender-neutral casting preferred. The voice should feel like a peer, not an authority.
- Pacing: faster in the first half (frustration), slower in the second (clarity).
- No vocal fry, no upspeak. Every sentence ends on a period.
- Record 3 takes: (1) quietly contained rage, (2) weary exhaustion, (3) matter-of-fact precision. The right cut will blend elements of all three.

## 4. MUSIC AND SOUND DESIGN DIRECTION

### Genre & Mood
**Genre:** Dark electronic / cinematic tension. Think: the score of a documentary about institutional failure, not a hype track. Closer to Arca or Burial than anything with a drop. The music serves the story; it does not perform.

### Structure Map

| Timestamp | Music State |
|-----------|-------------|
| 0:00–0:04 | **Foundation layer:** Single sustained deep sub-bass tone. Almost seismic. Barely melodic. Felt more than heard. Think: a room humming. |
| 0:04–0:08 | **Tension layer adds:** A high-frequency whine begins just under audibility, creeps up. Glitch SFX hit at 0:04. Three low percussive hits at 0:07 ("Not the media / Not the party / Not anyone"). |
| 0:08–0:10 | **Full tension:** Both layers audible now. Rising textural swell — not melodic, pressurized. |
| 0:10–0:11.5 | **SILENCE.** Hard cut to nothing. Full stop. No reverb tail. No fade. |
| 0:11.5–0:14 | **New layer enters:** Single clean synth tone, mid-range. Deliberate. A completely different sonic palette — the sub-bass is gone. This is clarity replacing pressure. |
| 0:14–0:17 | **Three chime tones:** Clean, high-pitched, one per teal confirmation line. Spaced deliberately. The synth tone continues building underneath. |
| 0:17–0:19 | **Resolution:** Music swells to its fullest point — a minor-to-major chord shift, sustained. Not triumphant. Resolving. Like pressure finally releasing. |
| 0:19–0:21 | **End card:** Music fades to low ambient hum. A final, single teal-frequency chime tone on the Poli logo. Then silence. |

### Specific Sound Effects
- **0:04:** Short digital glitch/artifact — sharp, electronic, 2–3 frames long. Not cute. Wrong-sounding.
- **0:06–0:07:** Three percussive hits — low, non-melodic. One per "Not ___" line. Like a gavel. Or a door slamming three times.
- **0:07 (red flash):** A single discordant high-frequency sting — one note, no reverb, harsh. Very short (4–6 frames).
- **0:14–0:17:** Three spaced chime tones — clean, precise, high. One per teal check line.
- **0:19:** A single teal-frequency sustained chime on logo reveal — this is the last sound in the ad.

### Music Licensing Notes
- Avoid anything with lyrics.
- Avoid anything that reads as partisan (no patriotic motifs, no protest-music energy).
- Original score is strongly preferred. If licensing: look for micro-budget original electronic composers, not sync library stock.
- Tempo: the first half has no fixed tempo — it's textural. The second half introduces a very slow pulse (around 60–70 BPM) that gives the clarity section a sense of heartbeat without becoming rhythmic.

## 5. END CARD

**Timestamp:** `0:19–0:21`

---

### Visual Treatment

**Background:** The deep dark charcoal/navy from the Poli section (#080C10). The sonar ripple from Shot 8 has one last slow pulse as the end card enters — then the background goes fully still.

**Logo/Wordmark:**
- `POLI` centered, in teal (#00F5D4), large — same treatment as Shot 6 but slightly larger.
- Beneath it, a thin teal horizontal rule (1px) extends from the P to the I, like an underline that's also a divider.
- Below the rule, in small white uppercase tracking-wide type:

```
              POLI
          ___________
     politicaltruth.com
```

*(URL treatment: lowercase, tracking tight, white, small — present but not screaming)*

**Tagline:**
Appears below the URL in white, sentence case, slightly larger than the URL:

```
See through it.
```

*(This is the tagline. Three words. It's a command. It's a promise. It's an invitation. It lands on the end card with the final chime tone — the two elements arrive together.)*

**Social Handle:**
Below the tagline, in a slightly smaller size, teal:
```
@joinpoli
```
*(or the actual handle — placeholder used here)*

---

### End Card Animation
1. Hard cut from Shot 8 to the end card background.
2. `POLI` wordmark fades up (12 frames) — the final chime tone fires here.
3. The teal rule extends left-to-right across the logo (8 frames) — like a scan completing.
4. URL fades up (8 frames).
5. `See through it.` fades up last (10 frames), slightly larger than all text below the logo.
6. `@joinpoli` fades up last (6 frames).
7. Everything holds for the remaining time — static, clean, confident.

**No animation loops. No pulsing. The end card just sits there, still and certain.**

---

### End Card Design Rationale
The stillness of the end card is intentional contrast with everything that came before it. The ad was moving fast — the end card does not move at all. That stillness reads as confidence. The brand doesn't need to perform. It's already made its case.

`See through it.` works because:
- It's a command, not a pitch
- It has double meaning: see through the lies, and see through using Poli as the lens
- It mirrors the emotional resolution of the last shot without repeating it
- A viewer who watched with sound OFF still gets the full emotional payoff from these three words

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Typography Specifications
- **Primary typeface (first half):** Bebas Neue or Barlow Condensed ExtraBold — uppercase only, white on black. Letter-spacing: -20 to -30 (tight). Line-height: 0.9 (aggressive stack).
- **Brand typeface (second half):** Same condensed face for main statements. For the sub-copy ("political truth. ranked. sourced." / teal lines), switch to a geometric sans-serif, regular weight — Neue Haas Grotesk Display or similar. Lowercase. This weight/case shift marks the cognitive shift from problem to solution.
- **End card tagline:** Sentence case, medium weight, tracking wide. A deliberate contrast to the all-caps aggression of the first half.

### Color System Summary
| Color | Hex | Meaning in Ad |
|-------|-----|---------------|
| Pure black | `#000000` | Problem state, raw |
| Near-black | `#080C10` | Poli space, clarity |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Truth, text |
| Red | `#E02B2B` | Contradiction, exposure, alarm |
| Teal | `#00F5D4` | Poli, evidence, clarity, brand |

**Rule:** Red and teal never appear in the same frame.

### Sound-Off Optimization
The ad is fully coherent with no audio:
- The three-act emotional structure is communicated entirely through text and animation style
- The beat of silence (black screen) becomes a visual pause — the empty frame reads as intentional
- The color system (white → red flash → teal) communicates the emotional arc visually
- `See through it.` as the final text provides emotional and narrative closure without audio

### Aspect Ratio / Safe Zones
- **Canvas:** 1080 × 1920px (9:16)
- **Text safe zone:** 72px margin on all sides minimum
- **UI elements safe zone:** Bottom 250px reserved for Instagram UI overlay — keep no critical text here (especially on Stories format)
- `@joinpoli` handle and URL may sit low but should remain above the 250px floor
