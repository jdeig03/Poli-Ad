The logo

A calm orb with a simple smile.

The orb is the brand's light: blue, purple and red, sharing the space equally. The smile is one clean white line. Together they say Poli is friendly, open and on every voter's side.

The idea

Left, right, and everything in between.

The three colours stand for every voter, so they always appear together and in equal measure. Blue and red are matched in brightness, and the light falls from straight above.
