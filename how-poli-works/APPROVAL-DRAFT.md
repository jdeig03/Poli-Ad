## Slide 1
**Title:** Meet Poli. Your personal town hall.
**Subtitle:** An AI Representative on your candidate's website that answers your questions using their own words.
**Mockup (SAMPLE CONVERSATION, "Maya's AI Representative"):**
- Voter: Where does Maya stand on property taxes?
- AI: Maya wants to hold the county property tax rate flat for the next two years and expand the exemption for seniors on fixed incomes. Want to hear how she plans to pay for it?

## Slide 2
**Title:** Ask anything, any hour.
**Subtitle:** No rally, no phone bank, no waiting. Just ask.
**Mockup (night, 11:04 PM):**
- Voter: It's 11 PM and I still don't know where Maya stands on school funding.
- AI: Happy to help, any time! Maya supports restoring the county's share of school building funds and adding counselors at every middle school. Anything else before you vote?

## Slide 3
**Title:** Answers in the candidate's own words.
**Subtitle:** Poli only uses what your candidate has shared. No spin.
**Mockup:**
- Voter: How did Maya vote on the 2025 transit levy?
- AI: Maya voted yes on the 2025 transit levy. Her campaign says she backed it to extend weekend bus service to Eastgate and Cedar Hill.

## Slide 4
**Title:** If it doesn't know, it says so.
**Subtitle:** Poli would rather say "I don't know" than guess.
**Mockup:**
- Voter: What's Maya's plan for the county fairgrounds?
- AI: I don't know yet. Maya's campaign hasn't shared a plan for the fairgrounds. I can tell you about her plans for parks and recreation, or you can contact the campaign directly.

## Slide 5
**Title:** Your questions shape the campaign.
**Subtitle:** The campaign sees what voters ask, so they learn what matters most.
**Visual:** Step 1 You ask ("How will you fix Route 9?") / Step 2 Poli answers (using only what the candidate has shared) / Step 3 The campaign hears what matters.
**Fine print:** Don't share sensitive personal details. Your personal information is never sold.

## Slide 6
**Title:** Your personal town hall is open.
**Subtitle:** Look for "Ask [Candidate]" on your candidate's website. Ask anything.
**Visual:** Poli orb + wordmark + askpoli.us (no button look).
**Footer:** Candidate not on Poli yet? Ask them to join at askpoli.us
