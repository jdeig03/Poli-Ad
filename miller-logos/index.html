Miller for Ward 3

Seven campaign identities: four for Glen Miller (including 2B, the refined flag-torch banner), three for Scott Miller. Each one is shown as a 3:1 banner (billboards, mailers, web headers) and as a standard 24×18 in. yard sign. Every mark uses flat colors so it prints cleanly in two or three spot inks, and every one hides the number 3 for Ward 3.

Before printing: Indiana requires a "Paid for by" disclaimer on campaign signs and ads. Add the committee's exact name in small type along the bottom band of the yard sign.
Print-ready PNGs (2×) and editable SVGs for every banner and sign are in the miller-logos/exports folder of the Poli-Ad repo.
